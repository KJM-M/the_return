################################################################################
INTRO
################################################################################

The crack of splintering wood. Fire. A scream. Black water closing overhead. Then nothing.

You wake face down in damp moss, drawing a ragged breath that tastes of earth and smoke. Roots cling to your arms, and black sap stains your skin. Your body aches as though it has been broken and carelessly made whole again. Something has brought you back, though you cannot remember what.

You force yourself upright and pull a fallen branch from the roots. The wood is old and weathered, but strangely warm in your grasp. Around you lies an island of black rock. The sea surrounding it is perfectly still. Beyond the shore, the world ends in white fog.

You have only a moment to act.

################################################################################

DESCRIPTION

CONNECTIONS

- To the <color=#8FA6C9><b>south</b></color>, a doorway leads back into the ruined chapel.

ITEMS

TheCrone1

Item Name:
crone

Description:
an old <color=#8FA6C9><b>crone</b></color> sitting beside the fire, her hood drawn low over her face.

TalkTo:
The crone raises her head slowly. Her gaze fixes on the amulet you carry. “So. The serpent is dead, and my own amulet has carried you through my barrier.” A thin smile appears beneath the hood. “How resourceful. Tell me, stranger—whose ghost are you chasing?”

Say “merlin”:
“Merlin.” The name sounds less like a word than an old wound. “Of course. It is always Merlin. Merlin the wise, Merlin the blessed, Merlin whose light the rest of us were meant to admire.” The crone gives a quiet, mirthless laugh. “I know how brightly he burned—and what he left in shadow.” She leans toward you. “But let us see what you truly know. What was the name of the land he called his own?”

Examine:
The old woman sits perfectly still, her face little more than a shadow beneath the hood. Her hands are thin and knotted with age, but there is nothing frail in the way she watches you. The firelight seems to bend around her.

THE END
################################################################################

DESCRIPTION

The Ferryman’s fingers close around the coins. He steps aside, and you climb into the boat. Without a word, he casts off. You look back once at the ruined chapel and the ghost land surrounding it, then turn toward the wall of fog ahead. The shoreline disappears behind you as the boat drifts silently into the unknown.
